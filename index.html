What the app does

Brett Covers Capital (operated by Brett Balquist) is a personal, single-user application that produces short-form startup-news videos ("Shorts") and uploads them — always as private or unlisted drafts — to the owner's own YouTube channel, @brett.covers.capital. The application reads public startup-news RSS feeds, drafts a video script, renders the video locally, and uses the Google YouTube API with the single scope youtube.upload to place the finished video in the owner's channel as a draft. Nothing is ever published publicly without the owner's explicit, manual approval.

The application serves exactly one user: its owner. It does not access any other person's Google account or data, collects no user data, and provides no public service.

Google user data

The application requests one Google OAuth scope only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are used exclusively to upload videos to the owner's own channel. No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or accessible by any third party. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
