Brett Covers Capital

Daily startup-news Shorts — private publishing automation for one person.

What this is

Brett Covers Capital is a single-user content pipeline. It reads public startup-news feeds, drafts a short video script, renders a Short, and uploads it — as a private draft — to the owner's own YouTube channel. Nothing is published without the owner's explicit approval. The only Google account it accesses is the owner's own, and the only scope is YouTube video upload.

What it does NOT do

  • It does not access anyone else's Google, YouTube, or social accounts.
  • It does not collect, store, or share data from any user — there are no other users.
  • It does not sell data, serve ads, or use Google user data for advertising of any kind.

Data handling

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine and are never shared, transferred, or used outside of uploading videos to the owner's own channel. No Google user data leaves the owner's machine.
