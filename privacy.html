Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Brett Covers Capital is a private, single-user content pipeline operated by its owner, Brett. This policy describes how the associated software handles Google user data.

What data is accessed

The software requests one Google OAuth scope: youtube.upload — permission to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. No other Google data (Gmail, Drive, profile, contacts, analytics, or any other service) is requested or accessed.

How data is used

  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner's own machine in a permission-restricted file.
  • Tokens are used only to upload video files to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • No Google user data is read, collected, mined, or transmitted to any third party. There is no server, no analytics, no advertising, and no data sale of any kind.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with any third party. There are no third parties involved.

Retention & deletion

Tokens persist locally until the owner revokes them or deletes the file. The owner can revoke access at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party apps.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/brett-b112/bcc-brand or contact the owner via the support email listed on the OAuth consent screen.
